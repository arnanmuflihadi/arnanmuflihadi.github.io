# AR(N)AN Urban Atlas

This repository combines a fictional tropical modern-fantasy adventure world with real professional research. Read the latest task scope and approval record before changing either.

- Use [fantasy-level-design](.agents/skills/fantasy-level-design/SKILL.md) before creating or changing RPG settlement layouts, building placement, public spaces or player routes.
- Use [game-visual-qa](.agents/skills/game-visual-qa/SKILL.md) before submitting a 3D environment or architectural phase. Include real browser screenshots, reproducible cameras, actual relevant regression results and measured rendering costs.
- Preserve functioning terrain/support, movement, jumping, collision, camera, renderer lifecycle, asset loading/cache, source licenses and approved implementation evidence. Keep Home/Work, all six research studies and `project=mbr` functional.
- Treat the first island as a compact RPG hub: skyport, town square, market street, Atlas Guild and highland lookout. The MRT station is optional; real Jakarta transit research remains available through the portfolio. Do not automatically reuse rejected Jakarta layouts.
- Distinguish technical validation, provisional internal visual observations and explicit owner approval. Passing tests or an internal score never grants human artistic approval.
- Complete authorized corrections and stop at explicit phase boundaries. Do not advance to another phase without owner authorization. A workflow/skill installation or read-only dry-run does not authorize game implementation.

Use the existing preview, camera presets, evidence records and tests. Keep helper scripts minimal and avoid installing unrelated dependencies or third-party skills. More-specific task instructions determine the current phase and scope.
