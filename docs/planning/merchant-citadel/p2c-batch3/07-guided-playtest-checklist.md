# 07 — Short guided playtest checklist (to be done by a person)

URL: `node merchant-citadel-p2a/server.mjs --port 4180`, then `http://127.0.0.1:4180/merchant-citadel-p2a/index.html` (default 30 m cell merging; `?merge=none` to compare). WASD move, Shift run, Space jump, Q/R turn the camera, drag to look.

1. **Arrival.** Spawn, walk south-west and south-east: do the outfitter (left) and the carrier's office (right) read as a gateway? Can you walk round both?
2. **The street.** Walk north to the junction. Does the cross street to the west end on a convincing shop (the potter's gold awning)? Walk to it.
3. **The artisan lane.** Enter from the south (smithy forge bay on your left), walk north past the smithy, the potter, the joiner. Is it clear which trade each is? Any place you get stuck or the camera jumps?
4. **Alleys and corners.** Walk into the 3 m gaps between the smithy and the potter and between the potter and the joiner; turn around in them. Is the character too transparent, and does that bother you?
5. **The workyard** at the north end of the lane, under the joiner's shed. Turn around; note the camera.
6. **Back to the Bazaar.** Walk lane to bazaar to both halls (Western Hall first: seal, lanterns, scales), back out to the main street.
7. **Paving.** Is the paving still too blue anywhere (hall floors in shade especially)?
8. **Feel.** Speed, turning around goods, any invisible wall, anything floating or intersecting.

Record: position (shown in the HUD), what happened, a screenshot.
