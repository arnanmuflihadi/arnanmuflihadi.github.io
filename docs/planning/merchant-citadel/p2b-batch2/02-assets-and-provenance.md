## 2. Verification and conditions

* **No new pack.** Every source is one of the packs verified in P1.2 (`../p1/04-source-license-register.md`: archive SHA-256, page, licence text). Nothing was purchased and nothing was downloaded in this batch. Rejected packs (Alefran, KayKit, Kenney, Poly Pizza singles) are not instantiated. **No first-island asset or architecture** is used (the kit is built only from the extracted packs under `.asset-inspection/`).
* **Quaternius** (CC0): the licence file ships inside the archive; no attribution required, crediting the creator in the portfolio is recommended.
* **Avramania** (CC0): `License.txt` in the archive and the creator's page. Its ReadMe states that no model was generated by an AI 3D or image tool; the models were built by scripts written with an AI coding assistant, which the creator discloses. Only the Food & Market and Village Builder packs are used.
* **Syntaxes of Play — open condition:** the CC0 statement is on the creator's page and **not** in the archive. A dated copy of the page must be kept with the P2 assets (P1.2 register). That copy is not in this package (it is not mine to fabricate); it is the one licensing item still owed. 24 models depend on it (counters, tables, kegs, shelves, food display, baskets).
* **Custom items** are listed separately from downloaded geometry in the registry (`sources` = `custom`) and are flat-colour authored meshes.

## 3. Missing materials and textures: found and resolved

| Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Cafe-pack FBX texture paths do not resolve | the loader re-links the same-named materials from the pack's own `all-merged.blend` (`citadel_lib._syn_materials`); all 24 SY models render with their textures (`foods`, `wood003`, `fabric026`, `table-cloth01/02`, `Wicker010B`, `vegetables`) |
| Vertex-coloured materials exported white (colour layer renamed, Color Attribute nodes not) | fixed in `p2a_kit_export.py`; pottery, coins and bottles now coloured (visible in the East Hall and the stall rows) |
| Primitive without a material (`A02c`, 80 faces) found by the GLB inspection | cause: origin-centred `FP-Chest_Wood`; replaced by a floor-origin chest; re-inspection: 0 missing attributes, 0 missing materials |
| 15 byte-identical Avramania palette textures and 2 identical normal maps | one image datablock per distinct file (52 reuses): **82 → 69 images** in the GLB; the loader's material cache then collapses the dependent materials |
| Metal/specular maps of the cafe pack are read as metal by glTF | constants (metallic 0.5 for listed metals, else 0; roughness floor 0.6): `production-config.json` |
| Scale | all models keep their source scale; placed buildings within 0.43 cm of the P1.4 envelopes; `FP-Banner_2_Cloth`, `SY-herb-drying-hanger` and every added asset were placed from their measured bounding boxes (origin conventions differ per pack: top, centre or floor) |
