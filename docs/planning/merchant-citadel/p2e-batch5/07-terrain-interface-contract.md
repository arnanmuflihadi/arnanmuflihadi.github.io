## 2. Buildable footprints and ground support

28 building entries (`buildings[]` in the JSON: id, node, plan position, rotation, wall footprint, envelope, top above ground, district). The envelope is the measured world Box3 including eaves, porches and balconies and **is what terrain must leave clear**. Ground under each building is the town datum except the hall, which stands on 1.01 m (the terrace) with its forecourt on 2.01 m. Requirements:

* Flat, level support at least to the **envelope + 0.5 m**. Towers (B01 26.79 m, B04/B15/B16 15.79 m) are the tallest masses; nothing in the terrain may rise within 6 m of their walls.
* **Ground-support envelopes for the hall and terrace:** two flat, walkable levels, the terrace (x ±14, n 62–74, 1.01 m) and the hall forecourt (x ±14, n 74–90, 2.01 m), joined by the hall steps (x ±4.8, n 72.4–74.0) and reached from the street by the terrace stairs (x ±5.8, n 60–62). The terrace's south edge (n 60–62) is open and 1 m high (a player can step off and drops to the street; tested); its two sides carry 1.0 m wall proxies at x = ±13.95 (n 62–74).
* **Cliff setback:** the town's outer buildings touch the following boundary lines; any cliff or drop must stay ≥ 6 m beyond them: west x −31.5 (E03 inn), east x 41.5 (B17 tea house), south n −6.7 (E01/E02 at the arrival court), north n 89 (hall tail). The edge beyond the hall (north) is **not** protected by any proxy; the island's edge rail is the island's job.

## 4. Orientations and future interfaces

* **Tea lookout (B17 / N16–N17):** the lookout terrace is at (36, 57) and the tea-house veranda node N17 at (36.2, 62.2) is on the south face of B17 (x 31.5–41.5, n 61.7–72.3), so the building faces back towards the bazaar and terrace. Recommendation, not a measured requirement: keep the sightline from (36, 57) to the terrace (0, 66) free of tall vegetation, and put any vista or drop beyond x 42.
* **Transport (N20):** a reservation at (0, −16), about 9 m south of the first buildings (E01/E02 end at n −6.7), on the main axis. Whatever transport arrives (skyport, gate) must connect at street level 0.01 at x 0 with ≥ 6 m clear width; no transport design exists in this package.
* **Arrival:** the preview spawns at plan (0, 4), looking north.
* **Hall rail:** the forecourt rail (n 76.2) currently closes the hall doors; if an interior is ever built, remove the five centre bays.
