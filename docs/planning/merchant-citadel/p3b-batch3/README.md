## What changed, per work package (before | after sheets in `visuals/sheets/`)

| WP | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **1 Ground and paths** | Paths and tea-row soil are no longer baked into per-vertex colours (which smeared at the 1 m mesh resolution). A 0.3 m **ground map** (two 576 kB single-channel textures: signed distance to the nearest path edge, tea soil-row weight) drives the terrain shader: path borders are crisp with a few cm of wobble and a trodden fringe, tea rows read as soil strips between grass, the old dark smears are gone. Geometry, walk mask and physics are unchanged. | `sheets/wp1-before-after.jpg` (8 cameras incl. 1.65 m eye height and a path-edge close-up) |
| **3 Cliffs and underside** | 74 faceted, terraced, strata-coloured **rock masses** (about 23.7 k triangles) half-buried in the cliff face (buttresses, ledges, overhangs) and hanging under the island, kept 21 m clear of the Cloudfall notch and below the rim by >= 1.2 m. The side and underside silhouettes are now irregular; the smooth radial-streaked bulb is covered. The shell triangle budget (+25 k) held. The crag dome was fixed in the follow-up pass below. | `sheets/wp3-before-after.jpg` |
| **4 Cloudfall** | The fall is four layered sheets (main, a wider fast front sheet, two thin side strands) with different speeds and phases, plus 150 spray quads falling along the face (one instanced draw) and the foam hard planks replaced by soft blobs; the faceted cloud cards are replaced by **soft cumulus puffs** (80, one instanced draw). Not done: a wider lip with a visible pool or ledge and bank vegetation (the lip view is unchanged apart from softer foam). | `sheets/wp4-before-after.jpg`, `after/wp4c-after-*.jpg` (clouds) |
| **2 Foliage** | The per-family tint table is **gone**: one global grade in the shader (saturation x1.25, albedo x0.72) for every family, per-instance brightness/hue jitter (instance colour) and wind sway (small plants stronger, trees and bark weak, displacement with the square of local height). One data fix remains: the pack's pure-white twisted-tree leaf *mask* texture still needs a green multiplier because it carries no colour. Colours stay close to batch 2 but with more variation. **Cost 0.0-1.0 ms** (measured A/B, 05) which misses the 0.5 ms target at the tea promenade and the arrival court. Not tested with `?tm=aces`. | `sheets/wp2-before-after.jpg` |
| **5 Animals** | Foot slide **measured** (see 02): the Quaternius clips do not plant feet, so rate tuning cannot reach the < 15 % target. Delivered instead: soft per-species size lift for the smallest animals (cat 1.28x, chick 1.47x, songbird 1.28x), instanced contact-shadow blobs, songbird hops and head flicks, turn-before-walk speed gating, a `dragonNear(x, z, r)` hook (stub, tested: 6 animals within 12 m fled). Not done: sleeping or resting poses, turn-in-place animation, IK foot planting. | `after/p3b3-wild-*.jpg`, `evidence/validation/p3b3-foot-slide.json` |
| **7 Camera tight spots** | **No change.** Crag upper 2.79 m, podium edge 2.79 m and summit 2.92 m (camera never collapses, character fade alpha >= 0.75) are accepted limits of the slope behind the player and the hall podium; the tea veranda minimum (1.49 m) occurs for the first 3.6 s of `tea_house_to_overlook` (frames 0-21 of 475) and recovers as the player walks away. A first attempt (boulder proxies below the camera low-prop height) changed nothing and was reverted; a ray test from the veranda start found no non-post camera obstacle, so the veranda cause is `UNVERIFIED`. | `evidence/validation/p3b3-camera-spots.json`, `p3b3-routes.json` |

## Crag + Cloudfall pass (follow-up after owner request "Fix crag + Cloudfall")

| Part | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Counting Crag** | (1) Rock faces of the terrain mesh get **crisp strata in the shader** (world-height bedding with noise warp, dark bedding lines, vertical joints) instead of the smeared vertical rust streaks (`?strata=0` restores them). (2) **23 outcrop masses** (7.4 k triangles) on steep, *non-walkable* riser cells only, 3 m+ from every path and clear of trunk and boulder proxies. Re-run on the final code (`evidence/validation/p3b3b-*.json`, `autotest-network-p3b3b.json`): **17/17 routes** (identical minimum cameras), 11/11 reachability, **65/65 edges** (0.409 m), 11/11 edge walks with 0 recoveries, camera spots unchanged (2.789 / 2.793 / 2.92 m), wildlife 600 s zero violations, spawn 27/27 legal (clearance 0.75 m; habitat home points moved because the new trees shifted the seeded scatter), songbird cycle in 4 habitats, 0 relocations. Performance (load about 5, same machine): 7.0-12.5 ms p50, crag bench 12.5 ms (was 12.2), +0.1-0.5 ms elsewhere; island GLB 6.9 MB (was 6.1).

## Checks that were actually run (final code, real preview)

* Regression: **17/17 landscape routes** (0 stuck, end error 0.47-0.50 m, limit 0.6), **11/11 reachability pairs**, **65/65 town network edges** (clearance 0.409 m as before), rim and edge walks 11/11 on the island with 0 recoveries, camera spot sweep as above, dragon-landing scan run. Console clean.
* Wildlife re-run: 600 s full-rate simulation zero violations, spawn 27/27 legal (min clearance 0.97 m), reactions per species, songbird perch > takeoff > fly > land > perch in 4 habitats, 0 relocations.
* Performance (1280x720, GPU-complete, load about 5.6): see [03](03-performance.md). All-on p50 rose by about 0.5-1.4 ms in gameplay cameras versus the Quaternius stage (3.4-12.2 ms overall); worst case the crag bench 12.2 ms; the island GLB grew from 3.2 to 6.1 MB.
* Foot slide measured per species (02). Not measured: any lower-end device, a phone, the `?tm=aces` look, wind at close range in motion (judged in stills only).
