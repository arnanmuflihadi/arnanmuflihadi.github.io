## Selection: D

- **D against B.** Enclosure rises from 51% to 55% and the bazaar edge from 38% to 46%. The civic "active" figure falls to 0 because the tower houses now face the bazaar, not the terrace; that is intended (a terrace has no shop doors on it).
  The cost is in the camera test: 40 of 84 directions fully clear against 52 of 92.
- **E against D.** The bend is the more characterful plan from above and gives a reveal at the junction. Measured, it loses: lane enclosure falls (artisan 64% to 54%, warehouse 46% to 42%), one workshop has no room on the inside of the bend, the camera test is worse again (37 of 84), and the warehouse swings away from the service court it was placed to enclose.
  The tower stays visible from the same 8 points, so the bend does not cost the landmark.
- **E is not dismissed.** If the owner prefers a bent street, the south-west quarter needs re-planning (workshop positions, the cross lane) and the whole southern quarter needs re-validating. E was measured as massing only and has not been built with assets.

D is selected because it delivers the visible changes (level, skyline, market street) on streets that are already validated, and because the one thing E adds is bought with losses in the secondary quarters that the brief asks to strengthen.
