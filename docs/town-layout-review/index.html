One centre. Five destinations. Room to explore.

Six temporary building envelopes define a compact tropical fantasy hub on the existing floating island. Greyboxes communicate height, enclosure and entrances; they are not finished buildings. No production settlement or new imported assets were added.

Three layouts on the actual terrain

A · Central Plaza Hub

Recommended. Short arrival sequence opens into a square; the Guild and four square/market edges share a centre. Small terraces cover dips without changing the terrain. A meadow bypass and the existing ridge/Cloudbreak circuit provide two return choices.

B · Terraced Hillside Town

Strong elevated skyline and scenic landings. However, 73.8% of sampled building/public-space footprint exceeds 35% terrain grade. The arrival climbs farther and repeated retaining structures would dominate the hillside.

C · Curved Market Loop

A legible circuit with discoveries around bends. Its 3,125 m² building cluster spreads destinations around a large interior; the Guild still needs a long separate spur. Less compact and harder to see the centre at arrival.

The player journey

Arrive at Pelabuhan Awan, see the Guild crown, and follow the rising paved approach between market frontages. The route opens into a 20 × 18 m square. The Guild is a short side approach; the west exit leads to the highland trail. Return through Cloudbreak or take the shorter meadow bypass. All six real studies remain on the Guild shelves, including Jakarta transit accessibility and MBR. No functional station is needed for this sequence.

Reference principles

Nintendo’s Skyloft overview supports the high-level idea of a settlement oriented by a prominent landmark. Our application is an original Guild silhouette and independent route graph, not a copied map. Folio’s zone system and interaction points informed proximity-based research access and an explicit separation between movement and content. Sky Isles was inspected as a reference for connected exploration destinations; its repository describes the feature, but its world source could not be retrieved. No reference code, models or proprietary assets were imported.

Remaining decisions

The envelopes have no interiors, finished facades or operating shops. Raised podium backs still need architectural treatment in R0-B2 if this plan is approved. The longest natural route includes existing steep ridge grades; it is playable with this controller but is not an accessibility claim. Landmark occlusion can change with final roof shapes and planting and must be retested. Human review of spatial enjoyment and composition remains essential.
