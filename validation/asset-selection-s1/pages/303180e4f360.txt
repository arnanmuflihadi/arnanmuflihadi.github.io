By MedicineStorm on 23 July 2025 - 4:30pm We're experimenting with cross-promotion with our various sister marketplaces, including: itch.io | Sketchfab | Freesound.org | Godot asset store BETA The following OGA artists host content on some of these other platforms. Check them out! 2DPIXX on Godot store! Tsorthan Grove on Godot store! Screaming Brain Studios on Itch.io! Croomfolk on Itch.io! GloryToTheMachine on Itch.io! Sprint Enterprises on Bandcamp! If you would like to cross-promote your content, just follow these steps: Have some assets hosted here on OGA. Submit some of your assets to one of the platforms listed above. The assets don't need to be the same as your stuff on OGA, but they must be shared under one of the free licenses accepted on OGA (or be compatible with such licenses): CC0, OGA-BY, CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, WTFPL, Public Domain, et cetera. This doesn't mean all of your content on those platforms must be under a free and open license, but the ones featuring a banner (below) must be. Display one of the banner images below and link back to Opengameart.org . When submitting assets to one of the above platforms, add a link to OGA's main page (https://opengameart.org), your OGA profile page, or one of your OGA submission pages. Display one of the banner images below as one of the media previews or in the asset page's description. Let us know! Post a comment below linking to your asset on the other platform(s). We'll add your information above and promote you here! NOTE: Due to the Terms-of-Use on these other platforms, you should only share assets where you have explicit permission from all contributing artists. If you plan to share assets derived from prior free works, you should obtain permission from the authors of all works that your asset was derived from before sharing it on the above platforms. If you are the sole author of your assets, no problem! Just give yourself permission to share your work! :D NOTE: OGA has no official affiliation or promotion with these platforms. The theme should focus on how phobias distort familiar environments, transform ordinary objects into threats, and create tension between vulnerability and resilience. Artists should illustrate unsettling worlds shaped by irrational fears, hidden dangers, and overwhelming sensations.
