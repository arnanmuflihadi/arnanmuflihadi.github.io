The localhost-only `wildlife=r0f2` layer uses the existing scene, cache, terrain sampler and update loop. The actual accepted-placement ledger reports 7 animals (3 foxes, 2 deer, 2 cows) and 35 cached fern instances; rejected candidates are omitted. Idle or eating skeletal clips play independently within 75 m of the camera; no wandering behavior has been implemented. The selected positions avoid the existing mapped trails, discovery pockets, obstacle boxes and steep local samples. This is not proof that every foot or animation pose is grounded. The visible landscape improvement remains limited, and the user's desired level of environmental richness has not been met.

## Environment art continuation — partial

The optional `nature=r0f2` subflag adds a separately disposable static habitat layer while retaining `wildlife=r0f2`. The final browser ledger reports 98 placed instances in five instanced batches: 23 forest, 6 valley, 51 meadow, 10 Cloudbreak and 8 ridge. It uses three actual Quaternius Ultimate Stylized Nature CC0 models (small shrub, flowering shrub and flower clump), plus the existing authored chiseled rock. The three glTF meshes contain 190, 274 and 136 triangles respectively. Source files and dependencies total 434,425 bytes excluding the source manifest; actual network transfer was not measured. `assets/game/nature-r0f2/source.json` records source and the creator-hosted license's unrelated heading. Cached geometry and textures remain library-owned; new cutout materials, rock geometry and instancing buffers are layer-owned and disposed by the layer.

Placement uses fixed candidate clusters and checks the current town height sampler, safe ground, obstacles, trail margins, observation pockets and wildlife home/endpoint clearances. Small plants and rocks have no new colliders. The ledger is `habitat-ledger.json`; it records accepted placements, not proof that every mesh or animal avoids clipping. Assets were converted from transparent blending to alpha-tested foliage through local material clones after an initial visual inspection showed translucent fragments.

Actual browser comparison evidence: `art-overlook-before-matched.png` / `art-overlook-after-final.png`, `art-ridge-before-matched.png` / `art-ridge-after-final.png`, and `art-forest-before.png` / `art-forest-after-final.png` use the existing fixed review presets at 1280×656, DPR 1, standard shadows. Foliage and small stones appear in the views, but the overlook and ridge changes remain subtle and do not establish the requested substantial art improvement. The forest review is partly obscured by existing trees. `art-island-after.png` is an additional actual browser aerial. Human artistic acceptance is pending.
